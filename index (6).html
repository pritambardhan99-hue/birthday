A Birthday Wish

Dear Subhajit Prabhu,

On this day, may Govinda walk beside you the way he walked through Vrindavan — unhurried, warm, and loved by everyone he passed. May your year be light like a peacock feather, steady like the Yamuna, and sweet like stolen butter.

Whatever the day brings, may you carry His calm in your chest and His smile on your face.

Radhe Radhe, and a very happy birthday.

A Blessing from Srila Prabhupada
